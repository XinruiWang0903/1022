# Raw source data status

No original raw source downloads were present in the two folders supplied for packaging.

The manuscript states that the analysis dataset was compiled from:

- Wind Database — third-party licensed database; redistribution terms require author confirmation.
- CSMAR Database — third-party licensed database; redistribution terms require author confirmation.
- China Urban Construction Statistical Yearbook.
- China City Statistical Yearbook.
- China Energy Statistical Yearbook.
- China Environmental Statistical Yearbook.
- Data Center of the National Bureau of Statistics of China.

The editor requested full raw datasets or, where redistribution is not permitted, stable access information and an explanation of the restriction. Before submission, the authors should add one of the following for each source:

1. The original raw file, if redistribution is permitted; or
2. A precise citation including edition/year, table or indicator, provider URL, access date, required subscription or data-use agreement, and any access cost; and
3. The code or a complete step-by-step record showing how each raw input was cleaned, merged, interpolated, and transformed into the analysis dataset.

The analysis-ready files are available under `data/analysis/`, but they must not be relabeled as raw data.
